## About me

I work where three fields meet: **petroleum engineering**, **robotics** and **AI**. My day job at Nabors is on drilling, fishing and completion operations, and my projects take that field knowledge into robots and machine learning models built for the rig.

- Engineer at **Nabors**, supporting drilling, fishing and completion operations for ADNOC
- **MSc in Petroleum Engineering** (in progress), Al-Azhar University, with a thesis on quantum AI for drilling operations
- Building robots in simulation: mobile robots, robot arms, quadrupeds and humanoids
